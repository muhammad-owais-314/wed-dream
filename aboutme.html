Who We Are

Built by Dreamers, For Dreamers

WeDream is Pakistan's premier wedding planning platform. We started in 2022 with a single mission: make wedding planning stress-free, beautiful, and accessible for every couple — regardless of budget or location.

Today we serve thousands of couples across 50+ cities, connecting them with verified halls, caterers, photographers, decorators, and more — all in one place.

Our Core Values

Trust & Transparency

Every vendor on our platform is manually verified. No hidden fees, no surprises — just honest, upfront pricing and real reviews.

Love-First Approach

We treat every wedding as unique. Our AI planner learns your style, budget, and preferences to create a plan that's truly yours.

Excellence Always

From our platform design to vendor quality standards, we obsess over details so your wedding day is nothing short of perfect.

Community

We're building Pakistan's largest wedding community — connecting couples, vendors, and planners in one thriving ecosystem.

AI-Powered Innovation

Our cutting-edge AI planner doesn't just suggest — it learns, adapts, and evolves with your vision to deliver smarter recommendations.

Local at Heart

Built in Pakistan, for Pakistan. We understand local traditions, budgets, and expectations better than anyone.
