Who We Are

Built by Dreamers, For Dreamers

WeDream is Pakistan's premier wedding planning platform. We started in 2022 with a single mission: make wedding planning stress-free, beautiful, and accessible for every couple — regardless of budget or location.

Today we serve thousands of couples across 50+ cities, connecting them with verified halls, caterers, photographers, decorators, and more — all in one place.
