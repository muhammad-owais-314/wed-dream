Information We Collect

WedDream collects information to provide better services to all our users. We collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, wedding date, and location when you register or book services.
  • Payment Information: Billing details, transaction history (processed securely via third-party payment gateways).
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our Platform, pages visited, features used, and time spent.
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, device model, and operating system.

We never collect sensitive data like government IDs or health information unless explicitly provided by you for special accommodations.

How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us create unforgettable wedding experiences:

  • Connect you with trusted wedding vendors and planners
  • Personalize your AI planning assistant recommendations
  • Process bookings, payments, and confirmations
  • Send wedding inspiration, offers, and important updates
  • Improve our Platform performance and user experience
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security of transactions

We may also use anonymized data for analytics and marketing research to better serve our community.

Data Protection & Security

Your trust is our priority. We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • 256-bit SSL Encryption for all data transmission
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure data centers with restricted access
  • Automated backup systems to prevent data loss

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login, bookings, and core functionality
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your language and display preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our Platform
  • Marketing Cookies: Deliver relevant wedding inspiration and offers

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect Platform functionality. We do not use cookies to track you across unrelated websites.

Information Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy. We only share your information in these circumstances:

  • With Vendors: When you book services, necessary details are shared with selected vendors
  • Service Providers: Trusted partners who help us operate (payment processors, hosting, analytics)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger or acquisition, with notice to users

We never sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Any data shared is strictly for providing or improving our services.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@weddream.com. We will respond within 30 days. You may also update preferences directly in your account settings.

Children's Privacy

WedDream is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us immediately, and we will delete the information. Parents or legal guardians may manage wedding planning on behalf of minors.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries where our servers or service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses approved by relevant authorities. By using WedDream, you consent to such transfers in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Questions About Your Privacy?

Our Data Protection team is here to address any concerns about your personal information. We respond to all privacy inquiries within 48 hours.
